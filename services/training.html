Training & Capacity Building

Virunga Digital Solutions – Empowering individuals and organizations with digital skills for innovation, career growth, and technological advancement.

Overview

We offer professional ICT training programs to improve digital skills and capacity building. Our training programs cover web development, software engineering, cybersecurity, cloud computing, networking, digital literacy, graphic design, and emerging technologies.

We also offer customized corporate training to help organizations strengthen technical capacity and improve productivity.

Through workshops, mentorship, and bootcamps, we empower learners with practical skills for employment and entrepreneurship.

We offer the following training and development programs:

  • Digital Literacy Training

  • Linux Administration Training

  • Network & System Administration

  • Cloud Computing
  • Career Development & Mentorship Programs

  • Software Development

  • Web Development

  • Cybersecurity Awareness Training

  • Internship & Projects

ICT Training Programs Overview

Program What You Will Study Duration Price (Rwf)/Student
Digital Literacy Training Our Digital Literacy Training equips individuals with essential ICT skills to confidently use computers, the internet, and digital tools in daily life, education, and work. The program provides step-by-step learning from basic computer operations to improved digital productivity. It covers key areas such as computer fundamentals, file and system management, Microsoft Office applications, internet use and safe browsing, email and professional communication, online collaboration tools, basic cybersecurity awareness, and simple troubleshooting skills. 1 Month 70,000
Cybersecurity Awareness Training Our Cybersecurity Awareness Training is designed to equip employees, students, and organizations with the knowledge needed to recognize, prevent, and respond to cyber threats. The training focuses on building a strong security culture by reducing human-related security risks such as phishing, password misuse, social engineering, and unsafe internet practices. 1 Month 50,000
Linux Administration & Network and System Administration Our Linux Administration & Network and System Administration services focus on managing and optimizing IT infrastructure to ensure secure, stable, and high-performing systems. We handle the installation, configuration, monitoring, and maintenance of Linux servers and network environments to support business, cloud, and enterprise operations while minimizing downtime. The services include Linux server setup and management, user and system administration, package updates, automation through scripting, performance monitoring, web and database server configuration, backup and recovery, and security hardening. On the networking side, we provide LAN/WAN setup, router and switch configuration, IP addressing, network monitoring and troubleshooting, VPN setup, firewall management, and overall network performance optimization. 2 Months 150,000
Cloud Computing AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, virtual machines, cloud storage, networking, deployment, security basics. 1 Month 80,000
Career Development & Mentorship Programs Our Career Development & Mentorship Programs provide personalized guidance and coaching to help individuals build successful careers in IT and professional fields. The program helps participants identify their strengths, set clear career goals, and develop the skills needed for long-term growth. Professional development including CV writing, Interview Preparation & Coaching, Job Matching & Career Guidance, communication skills, LinkedIn and portfolio building, job search strategies, along with personalized mentorship, career guidance, project reviews, coaching, networking, and personal growth. 1 Month 50,000
Software & Web Development Comprehensive training in software and web development, including programming languages (Python, Java, C++), algorithms, version control (Git), debugging, and application development, along with frontend and backend web technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, databases, APIs, and deployment practices. 3 Months 150,000
Internship & Projects Our Internship & Projects program is designed to give students and graduates hands-on experience in real-world IT and business environments. We bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry practice by involving participants in practical projects that build technical, analytical, and professional skills. The program covers structured internships, real-world software and web development projects, IT support, networking and infrastructure tasks, cloud and DevOps exposure, project-based learning, teamwork using agile methods, documentation and reporting, portfolio development, and continuous mentorship. 1 Month 60,000

These programs prepare learners for:

  • System Administrators careers

  • Cloud Engineer careers

  • Software & Web Developers Careers

  • Network Engineers careers

  • IT Support roles

  • Freelancing opportunities

Eligibility:

  • Open to students, graduates, and professionals interested in ICT skills development.
  • Basic computer knowledge is required for most programs (except Digital Literacy Training and Career Development & Mentorship Programs) designed for all.
