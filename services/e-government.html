Overview

At Virunga Digital Solutions, we provide professional support and guidance for accessing various E-Government and online services. We assist individuals, students, businesses, and organizations in completing online applications, registrations, document processing, and digital service requests efficiently and accurately.

Our services are designed to simplify access to digital platforms, save time, and ensure smooth processing of online services. We are committed to helping clients easily navigate digital platforms while promoting efficiency, accessibility, and digital transformation.

We Provide Support On:

  • Irembo services and online applications

  • Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA) services

  • Rwanda Development Board (RDB) online services

  • University and scholarship applications

  • Job application and recruitment portals

  • Passport and national document applications

  • Email account creation and setup

  • Online business registration

  • Digital payment and e-service support

  • CV writing and professional document preparation

  • Online appointments and service bookings

  • Document conversion, scanning, and online submission

  • General ICT and digital support services
