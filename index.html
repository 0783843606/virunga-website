About Virunga Digital Solutions

Virunga Digital Solutions is a Rwanda-based ICT and technology services company that provides innovative digital transformation, custom software development, and professional IT support services to businesses, government institutions, and organizations. The company focuses on helping clients modernize their operations through efficient, secure, and scalable digital systems.

Through its solutions, Virunga Digital Solutions aims to improve productivity, enhance service delivery, and contribute to Rwanda’s vision of building a strong, technology-driven digital economy.
