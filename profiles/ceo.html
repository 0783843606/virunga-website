About the CEO

MANIRIHO Jean Pierre is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Virunga Digital Solutions, established in 2025.

He also serves as a Senior Cloud Systems Engineer at the Rwanda Information Society Authority (RISA), a position he has held since 2023. Prior to this, he worked as a Network and System Administrator at Rwanda Polytechnic (RP), Ngoma College, from 2019.

He holds a Master’s degree in Information Technology from the University of Kigali and a Bachelor’s degree in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering from the University of Rwanda, College of Science and Technology.
