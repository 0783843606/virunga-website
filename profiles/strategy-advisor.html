About NAMBAJIMANA Frodouard

NAMBAJIMANA Frodouard is a results-driven Business Strategist and Project Management professional with more than 13 years of experience in institutional development, entrepreneurship, and business incubation advisory services. He also serves as a business development coach and mentor.

He has demonstrated strong ability in leading complex projects, designing organizational structures, and coordinating multi-stakeholder initiatives across both public and private sectors.

Currently, he works as an Institutional Functional and Organizational Structures Review Specialist at the Ministry of Public Service and Labour. In this role, he contributes to institutional functional reviews, human resource policy development, and governance improvement initiatives aimed at strengthening the capacity of public institutions to effectively deliver on their mandates.

Throughout his career, he has held various positions in both public and private sectors. These include roles as Business Incubation Officer and Entrepreneurship Development Officer at RP-IPRC Ngoma, where he led entrepreneurship and startup incubation programs, coordinated project implementation, and built strategic partnerships.

He also served as the Managing Director of IPRC East Engineering Company Ltd, where he was responsible for strategic planning, operational management, partnership development, project execution, and resource mobilization.

In addition, he has experience as an entrepreneurship trainer at Rusumo Secondary School, where he taught entrepreneurship and mentored students in business development and financial literacy.

Frodouard holds an MBA in Project Management, a Bachelor’s degree in Economics, and an Advanced Diploma in Entrepreneurship with Education. He is currently pursuing the globally recognized Project Management Professional (PMP) certification through the Project Management Institute (PMI).
